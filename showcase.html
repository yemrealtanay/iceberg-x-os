Our Initial Draft Approach

Initially, we designed a traditional, complex email client interface layout inside Lifesycle CRM.

But it was slow, complex, and did not fit the modern workflow.

What We Actually Needed

We took a step back and asked ourselves: what are the core business needs for email inside our CRM?

01

Track Replies

We must know when contacts respond to system-sent emails. This is critical to building a rich contact intelligence context.

02

Minimal Friction

Our current email modal is detailed but slow. We need an extremely fast, zero-effort way to fire off quick updates.

03

Centralized Hub

All client communication threads must be readable in one single, unified timeline.

04

Uzair Integration

Our intelligent assistant Uzair must be able to join, read, and process these incoming email exchanges.

The Solution

This is why we built
Email Chat

Email Chat provides fast, effortless email communication. We can still view the fully rendered HTML versions of emails, but we display the exact parts we need as clean chat text.

How It Works

The Inbound Token Engine

Every outgoing system communication carries an encrypted reply token to seamlessly map incoming replies back to the contact timeline.

01

Outbound Tagging

System automatically appends unique reply token headers and outbound hashes to the sender metadata.

02

Unique Token Envelope Address

reply+AAABTwAq3jkAAAAAFFwZxg.QOcN569i8wal2A@inbound.lifesycle.co.uk

03

Natural Reply Parsing

Client replies naturally from their native mail client. System captures the webhooks, matches hashes, and processes the mail body.

04

Timeline Integration & Conversation Creation

Inserts clean chat bubble on CRM timeline. Initiate new email chats from scratch directly with any contact.
